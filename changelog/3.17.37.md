# 3.17.37

Added
- Share now opens one sheet: invite someone to your session, or post a moment from it.
- Receipt: a card of what your agent did in a run, with files changed, tests passing and agent time.
- Timelapse: a run sped up into a short video, readable even from a wide desktop terminal.
- Meme: your agent's best line, with a caption. Offered right after a good one, or select any text.
- Phone shot: this terminal framed on a phone, as a post or a story.
- Wrapped: your week with agents, as stories.

Improved
- Everything is made on your device. Keys, tokens, passwords, emails, addresses, user names,
  your machine's name and session PINs are hidden before anything is drawn.
- On a phone the share sheet comes up from the bottom and stays in view with the keyboard open.
