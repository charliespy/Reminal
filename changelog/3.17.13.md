# 3.17.13

Improved
- New sessions get an 8-digit PIN. Sessions already running keep the PIN they have.
- If someone keeps trying wrong PINs on a session, new PIN connections wait a little longer each time, up to an hour, and your terminal tells you. Devices that already connected, and your owner devices, are never held up.
- Links from `reminal expose` carry a longer PIN, and each visitor gets a sign-in of their own.
- Wrong PINs on an `expose` link only slow down the address they came from, so other visitors are not kept out.
- A forced update only ever moves you to a newer version.
- Connections between your devices and your machines are sturdier, and libraries are updated.
