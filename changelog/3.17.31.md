# 3.17.31

Fixed
- Buttons no longer turn green after you tap them on a phone or tablet, and keep their own colour under the mouse.
- A Linux machine with no service manager, like a container or a small server, now comes back by itself after an update.
- Older Safari (16) can now own and connect to machines. A browser that can't says to update instead of showing an error.
- A machine updated in place no longer risks being run by its old version until a session ends.
