# 3.16.10

Improved
- Switching to a session, back to a recent one, and opening one are all faster.
- Switching back to a session shows its last screen right away while it reconnects.
- Busy terminals use much less CPU while they print a lot of output.
- After a burst of output, a session gives back the memory it took.
- Sessions wake the computer less often, whether they sit idle or someone is watching them.
- Revoking a device now signs it out of open sessions straight away, and changes their PINs.

Fixed
- After a network change the page reconnects by itself instead of sitting on a dead connection, and says if something you typed may not have arrived.
- A dropped connection comes back at once, and the page no longer stays on "Busy, trying again" after it has got through.
- The Reconnected note no longer pushes your prompt up a line, so what you type next stays on the prompt.
