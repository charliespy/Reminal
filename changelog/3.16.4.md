# 3.16.4

Improved
- Open a long session in a new tab and much more of its history is there: about three times as many lines as before, and you can scroll back through them. A tab you already had open keeps working as before.
- Someone opening your session no longer makes your terminal pause for a moment while they join.
- After a restart, a session no longer keeps holding memory it does not need, which matters most on small machines.
- If you run your own relay, the page it serves is about a third of the size, so it opens faster on a slow connection.

Fixed
- A session opened in a new tab no longer hides the last couple of lines just above the prompt.
- After a restart, a session's history is complete again instead of only the last screen or two. This was most visible on Windows.
- On Linux, closing an app while its window is mirrored no longer stalls the other apps on that screen.
- Switching to a session on another machine no longer briefly shows the previous machine's windows, and what you type during the switch goes to the session you picked.
