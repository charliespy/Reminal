# 3.16.11

Improved
- When the page drops a dead connection, it is back in under a second instead of up to ten.
- After you switch sessions, the terminal fills your screen straight away instead of drawing small for a few seconds.
- A session whose agent finished its turn while its own helpers still work in the background now shows as busy, not idle.

Fixed
- Typing fast no longer loses a repeated key: `44` stays `44`, and `$((` stays `$((`.
- On a first visit, the tour no longer takes your keys: what you type goes to the session, Enter included.
- A session you switch back to reconnects at once if its connection drops soon after.
